Archive status
--------------
This supplementary archive is supplied for editorial review, peer review, and
verification. No public open-source or open-data licence has yet been assigned to
the original code, predictor weights, or archived experimental records. External
redistribution therefore requires permission from the author unless a later public
repository record states different terms.

Third-party dependencies
------------------------
The archive does not redistribute Python, NumPy, pandas, PyTorch, Matplotlib,
ROS 2, Gazebo, RViz, or their transitive dependencies. Each dependency remains
subject to its own licence and installation terms. Exact package versions may vary
by platform; the release verification used the locally installed environment.

Predictor weights
-----------------
The three local predictor-weight files are included to support review and
verification of the reported experiments. Their SHA-256 digests are recorded in
SHA256SUMS.csv. Public redistribution is not granted by this review archive.

Evidence boundaries
-------------------
The RViz and Gazebo captures are authentic system screenshots. Numerical results
are derived from the supplied CSV records, not from screenshot pixels. The
ten-seed Gazebo boundary raw logs are not locally available; only their retained
summary and paired-difference tables are supplied.
